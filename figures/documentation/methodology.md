# Methodology

## Study Design

The study employed a descriptive cross-sectional research design to assess the quality of antenatal care services and client satisfaction among pregnant women attending selected primary healthcare centres.

## Study Population

The study population consisted of pregnant women receiving antenatal care services in selected primary healthcare centres.

## Sample Size

A total of 274 respondents participated in the study.

## Data Collection

Data were collected using a structured questionnaire covering:

- Structural quality of care
- Process quality of care
- Satisfaction with care
- Sociodemographic characteristics

## Data Analysis

Data were analysed using IBM SPSS Statistics.

The analysis included:

- Frequencies
- Percentages
- Mean scores
- Standard deviations
- Reliability analysis
- Correlation analysis
- Chi-square tests

## Ethical Considerations

Participant-level data are not publicly shared in this repository to maintain privacy and confidentiality.
