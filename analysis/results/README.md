# Results

This folder contains key findings from the antenatal care quality and satisfaction analysis project.

## Summary of Findings

### Structural Quality

Mean score: 1.35 ± 0.33

### Process Quality

Mean score: 1.36 ± 0.30

### Overall Quality

Mean score: 1.35 ± 0.28

### Satisfaction

Mean score: 3.03 ± 0.30

### Correlation Analysis

A positive and statistically significant relationship was observed between quality of antenatal care and client satisfaction.

r = 0.631, p < 0.001
