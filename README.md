# Antenatal Care Quality and Satisfaction Analysis

## Project Overview

This project presents a statistical analysis of the quality of antenatal care services and satisfaction among pregnant women attending selected primary healthcare centres.

The analysis was conducted as part of a healthcare research project, with the aim of examining different dimensions of care quality and understanding how quality of care relates to client satisfaction.

## Research Focus

The analysis examined:

- Structural quality of antenatal care services
- Process quality of antenatal care services
- Overall quality of antenatal care
- Satisfaction with antenatal care services
- The relationship between quality of care and satisfaction

## Dataset

The study included **274 respondents**.

The questionnaire collected information on sociodemographic characteristics, structural aspects of care, process aspects of care, and satisfaction with antenatal care services.

> **Data privacy:** The original participant-level dataset is not included in this public repository. Results and analysis materials are presented in a portfolio-safe format.

## Key Results

### Structural Quality

Mean score: **1.35 ± 0.33**

### Process Quality

Mean score: **1.36 ± 0.30**

### Overall Quality

Mean score: **1.35 ± 0.28**

### Satisfaction

Mean score: **3.03 ± 0.30**

### Relationship Between Quality and Satisfaction

Quality of antenatal care was positively and significantly associated with satisfaction:

**r = 0.631, p < 0.001**

This indicates that higher perceived quality of antenatal care was associated with higher levels of client satisfaction.
